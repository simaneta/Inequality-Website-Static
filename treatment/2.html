Elite Cues (T2)

Please read the following:

In an extraordinary move, over 250 of the world's wealthiest individuals, including prominent figures like Disney heir Abigail Disney, are calling for higher taxes on the super-rich.

These billionaires and multimillionaires, gathered under the "Proud to Pay More" campaign (proudtopaymore.org), argue that economic inequality has reached a dangerous tipping point.

They believe that taxing extreme wealth is not only a moral imperative but also essential for safeguarding societal stability and democracy itself.

They write: “Our drive for fairer taxes is not radical. Rather, it is a demand for a return to normality based on a sober assessment of current economic conditions.”

Despite their immense fortunes, these individuals assert that such taxes would not affect their quality of life but would significantly benefit public services and reduce inequality.

They write: “The true measure of a society can be found, not just in how it treats its most vulnerable, but in what it asks of its wealthiest members. Our future is one of tax pride, or economic shame. That’s the choice.”

Their plea to global leaders, especially at forums like the World Economic Forum, emphasizes that the time for action is now.

These billionaires and multimillionaires assert that they would be:
Proud to pay more to tackle extreme inequality.
Proud to pay more to help reduce the cost of living for working people. 
Proud to pay more to better educate the next generation.
Proud to pay more for resilient healthcare systems.
Proud to pay more for better infrastructure.
Proud to pay more for a green transition.
Proud to pay more taxes on our extreme wealth.
By taxing the wealthiest more, they argue, we can invest in a better future for all, transforming unproductive private wealth into a powerful force for the common good.
