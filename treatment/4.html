Framing Inequality Around a Wealthy Group (T4)

Now, we are going to ask you to reflect upon a group of the wealthiest people in society.

Please read the following information very carefully:

In 1990, the 25 richest people in the United States had an average net wealth of 2.5 billion dollars per person.

In 2024, that wealth has grown to an average net wealth of 84.4 billion dollars per person. In comparison, the latest estimate for the annual median household income in the US is $74,580.

Below, the average wealth of this group of people is represented graphically and compared to the annual median income in the US.

Please take a moment to understand how wealthy this group of people, on average, has become by scrolling to the bottom of this page.
