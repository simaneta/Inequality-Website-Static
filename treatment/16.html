Tax Avoidance by the Wealthy (T16)

Please read the following information:

Wealthy people tend to have high incomes. The top 1 percent of Americans with the highest incomes own 23 percent of all the wealth in the country.1

These very rich people are more likely than other Americans to avoid paying their taxes.2

For example, they might say on their tax return that their income was lower than it actually was. Using data from the IRS, researchers have found that the richest 1 percent are responsible for about 27 percent of all such “missing income” on tax returns.3

This leaves a large hole in the government’s budget. More than $160 billion is lost every year from taxes that the top 1 percent choose not to pay.4

Non-partisan tax experts agree that tax policy reform and increased enforcement would help reduce such tax evasion.5
