Graphic Wealth Disparity and Inherited Wealth (T13)

Grasping the magnitude of billionaires' wealth is hard.

As humans, we are not very good at mentally representing very big numbers.

In the following youtube video, the content creator Humphrey Yang represents one billion dollars using rice grains, where each grain represents $100,000. Please watch.

According to a recent report published by the media company Forbes in April 2024, "for the first time in 15 years, there are no self-made billionaires under the age of 30", which means that all the billionaires under the age of 30 have inherited their wealth.

The relevance of inheritance for younger generations' wealth is increasing. According to sources such as the UK-based Institute of Fiscal Studies, this is increasing inequality and damaging social mobility.
