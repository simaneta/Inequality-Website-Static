Illustrating Tax Disparities Between the Rich and the Poor (T20)

Before you proceed, please read the following information carefully.

The poorer half in the US wealth distribution owns only 1.5% of total wealth, whereas the wealthiest 0.1% own 17.8% according to the World Inequality Database (see visualization below).

This means that for every $100 owned by someone from the poorer half, someone from the wealthiest 0.1% owns $593,000.
