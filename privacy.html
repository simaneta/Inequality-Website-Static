Privacy Policy

1. About this website

Megastudy testing 20 treatments to increase support for redistribution is a research dissemination website developed and maintained by researchers affiliated with Aarhus University. It presents research summaries and materials related to a study on interventions aimed at increasing support for redistribution.

2. Website operation and data processing

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, with DNS services provided by Cloudflare and domain registration through EU.org. The website does not require registration or collect personal information through forms, surveys, or user accounts. We do not independently track or profile visitors.

When you visit the website, hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information, including IP addresses and request details, to deliver the website and maintain its security.

The website also uses external services:

  • Google Fonts provides the website's typography.
  • Altmetric and PlumX display research publication metrics.

Loading these resources may involve sharing IP addresses with the respective providers.

3. Research data

The website presents aggregate research findings and selected individual survey responses. For participants who explicitly consented to public disclosure, we display their age, political affiliation, US state, and response to a predefined question about wealth taxation.

No names or direct personal identifiers are published. The underlying research datasets are managed separately under the study's approved research and data protection procedures and are not available for download through this website.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use its own visitor analytics, advertising cookies, or behavioural tracking.

We do not intentionally store information in visitors' browsers to identify or track them. External service providers may use cookies or similar technologies when their resources are loaded.
